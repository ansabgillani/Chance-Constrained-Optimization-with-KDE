# Chapters 1–2 Restoration Design

## Goal

Restore the explanatory, mathematical, and literature context removed from Chapters 1 and 2 while keeping the thesis concise, evidence-traceable, and aligned with the current local implementation.

## Scope

Chapter 1 will regain the application bridge, the correctly ordered three computational difficulties, the parametric misspecification mechanism, the residual-space identity and benefits, explicit scope boundaries, and a numbered research-question list. Chapter 2 will regain a figure-consistent classical taxonomy, compact method-by-method comparison, cited KDE foundations, bandwidth and dimensionality caveats, a trade-off table, and a literature map connecting engineering constructions to convergence theory.

The old prose will not be restored wholesale. Unsupported universal violation claims, duplicated explanations, unconditional safety claims, and the incorrect shifted-kernel illustration will remain excluded or be rewritten with explicit qualifications.

## Evidence and notation rules

All displayed standard results will carry their foundational citations. The Gaussian SOC statement will state Gaussian uncertainty, affine decision dependence, covariance conditions, and the usual risk-level condition. Scenario guarantees will state independent/IID sampling, convexity, and support-rank assumptions. KDE equations will define the residual density, kernel, bandwidth, IID samples, and AMISE notation. The local shifted Epanechnikov surrogate will be identified as an analogue rather than a replication of Keil’s Split–Bernstein/MCMC/LGR workflow. Research questions will use a single four-question numbering consistently through Chapters 1, 4, 6, and 8.

## Figure rules

Captions will describe the actual generated artifacts. The taxonomy caption will match the deterministic/robust/stochastic/chance figure; the level-set caption will mention both the Gaussian convex and mixture non-convex panels; the bandwidth and staircase captions will match their generator outputs. The kernels figure will not be reintroduced until its support shift is corrected or the caption is weakened to a non-safety illustration.

## Expected result

Chapter 1 will be approximately 2,000–2,500 words and Chapter 2 approximately 3,500–5,000 words. Detailed numerical comparisons remain in Chapter 8. The thesis will compile without undefined references or duplicate labels, and a source-level audit will confirm that citations, notation, captions, and downstream RQ references are consistent.
